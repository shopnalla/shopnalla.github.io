Ordering & Returns

Because everything is made by hand after you order, our policies work a little differently to a regular shop.

Made to order & timing

We don't hold ready-made stock. Work on your piece begins only after you confirm your order, and the making time shown on each product is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Busy periods, unusual sizes, or custom color requests can add time. We'll always give you a realistic estimate before you commit, and let you know as early as possible if anything changes.

Making time is separate from shipping time.

Changes & cancellations

You can change or cancel your order free of charge any time before we start making it — just message us.

Once work has begun, we may not be able to cancel, since materials have been cut and worked. If we've already started, we'll discuss what's possible and any costs already incurred.

Returns & refunds

Each piece is made specifically for you, so we can't accept returns simply due to a change of mind.

We will repair, replace, or refund an item if:

  • it arrives damaged or faulty
  • it's materially different from what was agreed
  • the wrong item was sent

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we'll sort it out. Where a return is required, we'll cover return shipping if the fault is ours.

Sizing & handmade variation

For wearables we confirm measurements with you before starting, so please double-check them carefully — a garment made to the measurements you supplied isn't considered faulty if those measurements were wrong.

Because every stitch is worked by hand, small variations in size, texture, and color are normal and part of the character of handmade work. Yarn dye lots can also vary slightly from the photos.

Payment

We'll confirm the full price, including shipping, before anything begins. For custom and larger pieces we may ask for a deposit up front to cover materials, with the balance due before shipping.

Deposits cover materials already bought and worked, so they're generally non-refundable once making has started.

Shipping

We ship across Egypt, and internationally on request. Shipping cost depends on your location and is confirmed with you before your order is finalised.

Once a parcel is handed to the courier, delivery timing is in their hands. We'll share tracking details so you can follow it, and we'll help chase anything that goes astray.

Any customs duties or import charges on international orders are the customer's responsibility.
