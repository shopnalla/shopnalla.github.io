Good to know

Because every piece is made just for you, here's how ordering, changes and returns work.

Made to order and timing

We don't keep ready-made stock. We start making your piece once you've confirmed your order, and the making time on each product is our best estimate rather than a promise.

Busy seasons, bigger sizes or special colour requests can add a little time. We'll always give you an honest estimate before you commit, and let you know straight away if anything changes. Delivery time comes after making time.

Changes and cancellations

Changed your mind about a colour or size? No problem at all. You can change or cancel your order for free any time before we start making it. Just send us a message.

Once we've started, the yarn has already been cut and worked, so we may not be able to cancel. We'll always talk it through with you and find the fairest way forward.

Returns and refunds

Each piece is made especially for you, so we can't accept returns for a change of mind. If something isn't right, we'll repair it, replace it or refund you when:

  • it arrives damaged or faulty
  • it's noticeably different from what we agreed
  • we sent you the wrong item

Please message us within 48 hours of delivery with a few photos and we'll put it right. If a return is needed because of our mistake, we'll cover the return delivery.

Sizing and handmade charm

For clothes, we'll confirm your measurements with you before we begin, so please check them carefully. A piece made to the measurements you gave us isn't counted as faulty.

Every stitch is made by hand, so tiny differences in size, texture and colour are completely normal and part of what makes your piece one of a kind. Yarn colours can also look slightly different in person than on screen.

Payment

We'll confirm the full price, including delivery, before we start. For custom or larger pieces we may ask for a deposit to cover materials, with the rest due before your order is sent.

Because a deposit pays for yarn we've already bought and used, it usually can't be refunded once making has begun.

Delivery

We deliver all over Egypt and we're happy to send orders abroad on request. Delivery costs depend on where you are, and we'll confirm them with you before your order is final.

Once your parcel is with the courier, we'll share the tracking details and help follow up if anything goes astray. For orders outside Egypt, any customs or import fees are paid by the customer.
