Good to know

Because every piece is made just for you, here's how ordering, changes and returns work.

Waitlist and timing

Every piece is made by hand in our small studio, so new requests join a waitlist. Places are given in the order requests arrive, as long as the yarn and materials for your piece are available.

When it's your turn, we'll message you to confirm your order, its making time and an estimated delivery date. The making time depends on what you've ordered and starts once your deposit is paid.

If a yarn you've chosen isn't available, we'll suggest a similar colour or keep your place until it's back in stock.

You're welcome to leave the waitlist any time before your order is confirmed. There's nothing to pay and no need to explain.

Changes and cancellations

Changed your mind about a colour or size? No problem at all. Just message us before your deposit is paid and we'll happily update your request.

Once we've started, the yarn has already been bought and worked, so the deposit can't be refunded. We'll always talk it through with you and find the fairest way forward.

Returns and refunds

Each piece is made especially for you, so we can't accept returns for a change of mind. If something isn't right, we'll repair it, replace it or refund you when:

  • it arrives damaged or faulty
  • it's noticeably different from what we agreed
  • we sent you the wrong item

Please message us within 48 hours of delivery with a few photos and we'll put it right. If a return is needed because of our mistake, we'll cover the return delivery.

Sizing and handmade charm

For clothes, we'll confirm your measurements with you before we begin, so please check them carefully. A piece made to the measurements you gave us isn't counted as faulty.

Every stitch is made by hand, so tiny differences in size, texture and colour are completely normal and part of what makes your piece one of a kind. Yarn colours can also look slightly different in person than on screen.

Payment

Once we confirm your order, you have 24 hours to pay the 200 EGP deposit by InstaPay, mobile wallet or bank transfer. It covers the yarn and materials for your piece. If it isn't paid in time, we offer the place to the next person on the waitlist to keep things fair for everyone, and you're welcome to rejoin.

The rest is paid when your order is delivered, by InstaPay or cash on delivery. Your deposit comes off the total, so a 1,200 EGP piece means 200 EGP now and 1,000 EGP plus delivery when it arrives.

Because the deposit pays for materials bought and used for your piece, it can't be refunded once making has begun.

Delivery

We deliver all over Egypt. Delivery fees depend on your area, and we'll confirm them with you before we start.

To make sure your order reaches you on time, we ask for your full address and a Google Maps location link. We'll let you know when your parcel is on its way and help follow up if anything goes astray.
